# Investigations

Closed method studies. Each directory is a record of a question asked on the
TTM-001 and TTM-003 data in 2026, the scripts and notebooks that answered it, and the
findings document that holds the answer. The decision each one produced lives
in the library or the workspace pins; the directory is not maintained as
runnable code. Paths inside the scripts and notebooks are literal `tononi-2`
paths as they were run (`/nvme/neuropixels/tetrode_data`,
`/Volumes/neuropixel_archive/tetrode_data`, `/nvme/neuropixels/tmp`; the raw
sessions moved to `/Volumes/tetrode/<SUBJECT>/` on 2026-10-19 and the old
location was emptied the next day), and the
READMEs' `cd gfys_workspace && uv run ...` instructions describe the
environment of the time; run anything from this workspace's environment
instead.

| [`per-tetrode-matching/`](per-tetrode-matching/README.md) | Does circus-omp run on each tetrode's channels on its own keep clean tetrodes' spikes during multi-tetrode artifacts, and what does it change in clean data? | It keeps them (42,730 TT5 to TT12 spikes in 60 s of the unmasked event against 4,943 joint), but on clean data it adds 25 % spikes, mostly poor fits and events shared with other tetrodes, which the joint matcher's competition across tetrodes kept out; amplitude and failure settings do not restore joint's precision. Over 14 carry-forward windows the small units' templates shrink toward noise window by window (TT9: 16,100 clean spikes in window 0, 2.6 to 3.0 M in window 6, median trough 3.0 MAD), masked or not | `ttm.matching`, `mp_baseline.py --per-tetrode-matching` (off by default, not for production) |
| [`carry-bakeoff/`](carry-bakeoff/README.md) | On hybrid ground truth in TTM-003's first 6 h, and over its 48 h: can circus-omp per tetrode beat joint matching in the carry-forward, and how should the carry rebuild its templates? | Whitening each tetrode, admission in noise units independent of the template's scale (snr >= 4.5, relative floor 0.5 of the median fit, cosine of the neighbour-subtracted snippet >= 0.5, refractory 1.5 ms, the tetrode's own bad periods dropped) and a rebuild by a blend of 0.2 toward the window's clean lead spikes raise small-unit F1 from June's 0.38 to 0.79. With them, joint and per-tetrode matching tie on F1: per tetrode has more recall, more half-size in-pulse spikes and needs no mask; joint has more precision and is four times faster. June's full replacement ratchets templates up and moves spike times off the trough. Over 48 h the per-tetrode carry keeps each light-driven cell on one unit; its few-unit tetrodes' units grow 10 to 100 times by fitting events shared across tetrodes, which joint matching's competition kept out (`docs/reports/2026-10-29_ttm003_carry_bakeoff.md`) | Not yet in the library |
| [`common-reference/`](common-reference/README.md) | Why the global median reference leaves part of an event on all 64 wires on TTM-003's quiet tetrodes, and what referencing differently costs | Each wire is sampled at its own offset from the median: a ramp of about 1 us per channel within each bank of 32, the same in TTM-001 to TTM-003, plus electrode-specific offsets that drift by up to 13 us over 48 h. Shifting each wire by its offset and subtracting its gain times the median removes 70 to 90 % of the remainder; the cross-tetrode median does not help and costs 47 s against 8 s per 60 s. The global median stays (user, 2026-11-02); a binary with the shift is not built | Not yet in the library |
| [`wobble-per-tetrode-ttm001/`](wobble-per-tetrode-ttm001/README.md) | Should the wobble against circus-omp comparison have run both matchers per tetrode? On clean TTM-001 stretches, how far does each move from joint to per tetrode, and does their order change? | Wobble does not move (280,469 spikes joint, 280,484 per tetrode in 300 s, 62 found only per tetrode); circus-omp gains 11 to 13 %, mostly small poor fits on small-template tetrodes (median cosine 0.670, trough 5.9 MAD). No measure changes order between the joint and per-tetrode pairs; conjecture: the span test's wobble arms already stand for per-tetrode wobble. `find_spikes_per_group` runs wobble with nothing special | Nothing changes in the pipeline |

threads together because they share `_track_eval.py`, `_wobble_eval.py`,
`_assignment_eval.py` and `_scoreboard.py`; three tests under
`packages/ttm/tests/` import those helpers. The UnitRefine
scripts (46-50) import only the library and sit on their own.
